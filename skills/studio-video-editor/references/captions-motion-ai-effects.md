# Captions, motion & AI effects (Version 2 only)

## Subtitles & on-screen text
Subtitles are standard whenever the video has speech, unless the creator chose "No subtitles" — **but only after the montage is explicitly approved.** Start from the corrected Whisper `.srt`, imported as a caption track directly into the editing project (not via another app), then style it. No speech or music only → no subtitles.
- Subtitle style from the Editing Playbook, matching the creator's recent videos (fonts, colors, position).
- Accurate sync, mobile readability, never covering faces or key visuals, platform safe areas, consistent position and size, transcription errors corrected.
- **Language:** the content language from the Creator Profile. Preserve the creator's natural dialect, vocabulary, expressions and tone — don't "correct" them into formal language. If the spoken language differs, subtitle in that language unless told otherwise.
- Selective emphasis on key words, standout lines, strong reactions and key messages. Avoid needless word-by-word animation.

## Graphics, animations & effects
After approval you may propose and add animated titles, motion graphics, signature transitions, animated text, tracking graphics, comedic effects and purposeful zooms. Every animation must serve storytelling, clarity, engagement or recognizability. No clutter; don't use effects just because they exist.

## AI effects (only if an AI effects tool is listed in the Creator Profile)
**Strict gate:** only after the creator explicitly approved Version 1. Before that: no generating, testing, preparing, purchasing or implementing.

After approval you may experiment with AI animations, cinematic camera moves, scene transitions, visual transformations, surprising or surreal moments, intros and outros — when it genuinely adds value. Priorities: entertainment value → storytelling and impact → authenticity → consistent look → engagement. Don't make every video look AI-generated. Preserve the creator's natural appearance and identity; never alter real people's faces/bodies unnecessarily or misrepresent real events. Stay consistent with the creator's recognizable look (Editing Playbook).

Workflow: review the approved timeline → find moments that benefit → choose capabilities → develop concepts → generate (only with verified access) → review quality → import into the existing project → integrate naturally → save → tell the creator. Never export automatically.

Limits: no paid credits/subscriptions without authorization · no uploads of footage with identifiable third parties without permission · don't change the approved storyline · don't replace real footage unnecessarily. If the tool is unavailable, say so and continue with the editing software's own capabilities. Never claim an effect was generated or imported unless it actually was.

Record approved and rejected AI effects in the Editing Playbook.
