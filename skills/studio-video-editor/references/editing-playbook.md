# Editing Playbook

Maintain a persistent document **`EDITING-PLAYBOOK.md`** in the creator's content workspace (a starter template ships with the Creator Studio package). If it doesn't exist yet, start it from the template after the first approved video, filled with what you learned from the creator's published videos and feedback. It is the long-term creative reference for all edits.

Structure:
1. Visual style — subtitle font, colors, logo/end card, recurring graphics
2. Editing style — pacing, cuts, transitions, zooms, storytelling techniques
3. Personality — examples of timing, reactions, authentic moments that worked
4. Subtitles — typography, position, highlighting, animation, language rules
5. Sound design — music preferences, SFX, mixing standards
6. Approved video references — edits the creator particularly likes
7. Creative feedback — recurring corrections, rejected ideas, lessons learned
8. Platform guidelines — Instagram, TikTok, Shorts, long-form
9. AI effects — approved and rejected styles
10. Storytelling & chronology — hook, structure and continuity preferences

Learning process after each approved project: review feedback → identify reusable preferences → update the playbook when write access exists → keep existing preferences unless a change is justified → separate permanent rules from one-off decisions. Consult the latest playbook before every new project. Without write access, prepare the proposed updates and say clearly that they're not saved yet.
