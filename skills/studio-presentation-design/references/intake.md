# Interactive presentation briefing
Ask in one compact interactive form when supported; otherwise send concise numbered choices. Skip answered questions.

1. **Type:** Sponsorship/partnership pitch · Commercial proposal · Brand presentation · Performance report · Event concept · Media kit · Internal update · Other (free text).
2. **Audience and goal:** Who will see it? What decision or action should it drive?
3. **Source materials (always ask):** Do you have specific photographs, text, statistics, spreadsheets, documents, previous decks, logos or references to use? Allow multiple selections and uploads.
4. **Length:** 5–8 slides · 9–15 · 16–25 · Let Claude decide · Custom.
5. **Style:** Bold/impactful · Premium/minimal · Energetic · Editorial · Corporate/data-driven · Surprise me.
6. **Language:** Dutch · English · Other.
7. **Output:** Editable PowerPoint · Google Slides (if available) · PDF · Multiple formats.
8. **Deadline / non-negotiables:** Optional free text.

If a logo or photos are needed and not accessible, ask for them. Do not block a simple outline when design assets are unavailable; explain what is provisional.
